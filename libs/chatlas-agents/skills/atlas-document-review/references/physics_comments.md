# Physics Review Guidance

Source: `atlas_review_resources/physics_comments.md`

- Focus on constructive, specific, and actionable comments on physics content and clarity.
- Use particle-physics and ATLAS knowledge to identify gaps, inconsistencies, and missing references.
- Check logical flow and highlight unsupported or unclear arguments.
- Suggest improvements to explanations of complex concepts.
- Prefer established physics knowledge, ATLAS public results, and standard high-energy-physics practice as support for comments.
