# ATLAS Document Review Checklist

- Check the document type, target audience, and intended claim level.
- Verify that key physics statements are supported by the text, figures, or references.
- Check definitions of acronyms, variables, and observables on first use.
- Compare numbers across abstract, body, tables, figures, captions, and conclusions.
- Check that uncertainties, selections, and assumptions are described consistently.
- Confirm citations are present for nontrivial claims and methods.
- Look for ATLAS collaboration terminology, tone, and formatting issues.
- Flag ambiguous language, overstatement, and missing caveats.
- Separate factual or technical problems from style-only edits.
- Suggest precise replacement text when a fix is straightforward.
