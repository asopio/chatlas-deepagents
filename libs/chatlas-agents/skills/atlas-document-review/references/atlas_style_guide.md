# ATLAS Style Guide

Source: `atlas_review_resources/atlas_style_guide.md`

Use this file as the primary style reference for ATLAS collaboration documents.

## Key points

- Match ATLAS terminology consistently.
- Keep the title concise, descriptive, and aligned with ATLAS conventions.
- Write abstracts as a single concise paragraph with the essential measurement, dataset, method, and conclusion.
- Keep the introduction brief and focused on the measurement, motivation, and existing knowledge.
- Define non-standard jargon and abbreviations unless they are widely known.
- Keep notation and terminology consistent throughout the document.
- Prefer clear, direct scientific prose.
- Follow ATLAS typography and LaTeX conventions in figures, tables, references, units, and symbols.
