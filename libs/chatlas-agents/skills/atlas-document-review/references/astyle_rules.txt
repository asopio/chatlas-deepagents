# ATLAS Style Checker Rules

Source: `atlas_review_resources/astyle_rules.txt`

This file preserves the style-check vocabulary and common issues used by the ATLAS style checker.

## Representative checks

- Prefer `use` over `utilize`.
- Prefer `uses` over `utilizes`.
- Prefer direct wording over inflated or vague phrasing.
- Watch for double words and duplicated phrases.
- Use consistent spelling, punctuation, quotation marks, and hyphenation.
- Use proper LaTeX formatting for math subscripts, ranges, units, and quotes.
- Avoid common grammar slips such as double articles, stray periods, and sentence fragments.
